Pure masala, ground fresh

Vimal Spicy Food Masala offers everyday spices and ready-blend masalas for the kitchen – turmeric, chilli and coriander powders, sabzi masala, paneer masala, chaat, tea masala and more.

Spices are ground fresh, so the colour, aroma and flavour reach your kitchen as nature intended. यहाँ सभी प्रकार के मसाले हाथों-हाथ पीस कर दिए जाते हैं।

Our purpose is simple: honest quality and a taste every family can trust – from children to elders. Your satisfaction is how we measure every pack.
